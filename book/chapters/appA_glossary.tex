\chapter{Glossary of Machine Learning and TinyML Terms}\label{app:glossary}

Throughout this textbook, we have introduced hundreds of foundational concepts spanning linear algebra, statistical learning theory, deep neural network architectures, signal processing, embedded systems, and MLOps.

This comprehensive glossary serves as an authoritative, alphabetical reference for every major technical term used in the book. Each entry provides a concise, plain-language definition that explains the core intuition, its mathematical or hardware significance, and its concrete application in real-world engineering.

\begin{keyidea}[The Value of Terminology Precision]
Engineering and scientific progress rely on shared, unambiguous definitions. In interdisciplinary fields like TinyML---which bridges the gap between software data science and physical electrical engineering---using precise terminology prevents costly communication failures, software bugs, and hardware design flaws.
\end{keyidea}

\begin{analogy}[The Scientific Rosetta Stone]
Imagine entering a foreign workshop where veteran machinists use terms like \emph{collet}, \emph{tramming}, \emph{backlash}, and \emph{gcode}. Without understanding their lexicon, you cannot operate their machines safely or comprehend their instructions. This glossary is your Rosetta Stone: whenever you encounter an unfamiliar term in an academic paper, an engineering meeting, or a hardware datasheet, return here to quickly decode its meaning and operational context.
\end{analogy}

\begin{iotbox}[Embedded and TinyML Vocabulary]
Terms marked with \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\bfseries [TinyML \& Hardware]} represent the specialized language of resource-constrained edge systems. Understanding the precise distinction between \textbf{SRAM} (volatile runtime activation memory) and \textbf{Flash ROM} (static weight storage), or between \textbf{Active Current} and \textbf{Quiescent Deep Sleep Current}, is essential when engineering battery-powered smart devices designed to operate autonomously for years.
\end{iotbox}

\vspace{1em}

\section*{A}
\begin{description}[leftmargin=!,labelwidth=4.5cm,itemsep=1.5ex]
\item[\textbf{Ablation Study}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Research]}]: An experimental procedure where individual components, loss terms, or features are systematically removed one by one to quantify their isolated contribution to overall performance.
\item[\textbf{Accuracy}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Evaluation]}]: The proportion of total predictions that are correct: $(TP + TN) / (TP + TN + FP + FN)$. Misleading on imbalanced datasets where predicting the majority class yields high scores.
\item[\textbf{Activation Function}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Deep Learning]}]: A non-linear mathematical operation (e.g., ReLU, Sigmoid, GELU) applied to a neuron's weighted sum, enabling networks to learn non-linear decision boundaries.
\item[\textbf{Active Current}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[IoT \& Hardware]}]: The electrical current consumed by a microcontroller while CPU cores and peripherals are actively computing inferences ($30\,\text{mA}$ to $240\,\text{mA}$ on an ESP32 at $240\,\text{MHz}$).
\item[\textbf{Adam Optimizer}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Deep Learning]}]: An adaptive optimization algorithm tracking exponentially decaying averages of past gradients (first moment) and squared gradients (second moment) for stable, rapid convergence.
\item[\textbf{Affine Quantization}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[TinyML]}]: An asymmetric integer quantization mapping continuous floats $r$ to discrete 8-bit integers $q$ via scale $S$ and zero-point $Z$: $q = \text{round}(r / S) + Z$.
\item[\textbf{Aliasing}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Signal Processing]}]: An irreversible distortion occurring when an analog signal is sampled below twice its highest frequency ($f_s < 2 f_{\max}$), causing high frequencies to fold into false low frequencies.
\item[\textbf{Analog-to-Digital Converter (ADC)}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[IoT \& Hardware]}]: A hardware peripheral converting continuous analog voltages into discrete binary integers (e.g., a 12-bit ADC maps $0\,\text{V}--3.3\,\text{V}$ to integers $0$--$4095$).
\item[\textbf{Anomaly Detection}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Machine Learning]}]: The identification of rare, abnormal observations deviating from baseline normal behavior, commonly solved on edge devices using autoencoder reconstruction error thresholds.
\item[\textbf{Area Under the ROC Curve (ROC-AUC)}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Evaluation]}]: A threshold-independent metric measuring the probability that a model ranks a random positive instance higher than a random negative instance ($0.5 = \text{random}$, $1.0 = \text{perfect}$).
\item[\textbf{Autoencoder}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Deep Learning]}]: An unsupervised neural network with an encoder compressing inputs into a lower-dimensional latent bottleneck and a decoder reconstructing the original signal, used for edge anomaly detection.
\end{description}

\section*{B}
\begin{description}[leftmargin=!,labelwidth=4.5cm,itemsep=1.5ex]
\item[\textbf{Backpropagation}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Deep Learning]}]: The foundational algorithm computing partial derivatives of the loss function with respect to all network weights by applying the calculus chain rule from output backwards.
\item[\textbf{Bagging}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Classical ML]}]: Bootstrap Aggregating: an ensemble method training independent base models (e.g., decision trees) on bootstrap samples with replacement, averaging outputs to dramatically reduce variance.
\item[\textbf{Bandwidth}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[IoT \& Networking]}]: The data transfer rate across a network. Transmitting raw high-frequency sensor telemetry saturates bandwidth, motivating on-device TinyML inference.
\item[\textbf{Batch Normalization}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Deep Learning]}]: A layer normalizing hidden activations across mini-batches to zero mean and unit variance, stabilizing deep network training and mitigating internal covariate shift.
\item[\textbf{Bayes' Theorem}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Mathematics]}]: A fundamental probability formula updating conditional beliefs given evidence: $P(A|B) = \frac{P(B|A) P(A)}{P(B)}$, forming the foundation of Bayesian classification.
\item[\textbf{Bessel's Correction}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Statistics]}]: Using $N - 1$ instead of $N$ in sample variance calculations ($s^2 = \frac{1}{N-1} \sum (x_i - \bar{x})^2$), correcting downward bias when estimating population variance.
\item[\textbf{Better BibTeX (BBT)}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Research Tools]}]: A Zotero extension generating deterministic citation keys and auto-exporting live \code{.bib} files with preserved capitalization for \LaTeX{} publishing.
\item[\textbf{Bias-Variance Tradeoff}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Classical ML]}]: The fundamental trade-off where prediction error decomposes into bias (underfitting), variance (overfitting), and irreducible noise: $\text{MSE} = \text{Bias}^2 + \text{Variance} + \sigma_\epsilon^2$.
\item[\textbf{Binary Cross-Entropy}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Deep Learning]}]: The standard loss function for two-class classification: $\mathcal{L} = -\frac{1}{N} \sum [y \ln(\hat{p}) + (1-y) \ln(1-\hat{p})]$, penalizing overconfident incorrect predictions.
\item[\textbf{Bit-width}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[TinyML \& Hardware]}]: The number of binary bits representing numeric values in memory (e.g., 32-bit float vs. 8-bit integer). Lower bit-widths slash memory usage and energy consumption.
\item[\textbf{Boosting}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Classical ML]}]: A sequential ensemble method training weak learners iteratively, with each new model focusing computational capacity on correcting the residual errors of preceding models.
\item[\textbf{Box-Cox Transformation}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Data Engineering]}]: A parametric power transform stabilizing variance and mapping skewed positive features into an approximately normal distribution: $y^{(\lambda)} = (y^\lambda - 1)/\lambda$.
\end{description}

\section*{C}
\begin{description}[leftmargin=!,labelwidth=4.5cm,itemsep=1.5ex]
\item[\textbf{Calibration}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Evaluation]}]: The property where predicted probabilities reflect empirical real-world frequencies (e.g., samples predicted at $80\%$ confidence are correct $80\%$ of the time).
\item[\textbf{Catastrophic Forgetting}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Deep Learning]}]: The tendency of neural networks to completely erase previously learned skills upon being trained sequentially on a new task or distribution.
\item[\textbf{Categorical Cross-Entropy}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Deep Learning]}]: The standard loss function for multi-class classification: $\mathcal{L} = -\sum_{k=1}^K y_k \ln(\hat{p}_k)$, measuring divergence between true one-hot targets and Softmax probabilities.
\item[\textbf{Centroid}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Classical ML]}]: The geometric center or mean coordinate vector of a cluster of data points in multi-dimensional feature space, updated iteratively in K-Means clustering.
\item[\textbf{Channel Pruning}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[TinyML]}]: A structured model compression technique removing entire convolutional channels from weight tensors, directly reducing memory buffer sizes and runtime FLOPs.
\item[\textbf{CI/CD}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[MLOps]}]: Continuous Integration and Continuous Deployment: automated pipelines executing unit tests, code linting, and container builds on every Git commit.
\item[\textbf{Concept Drift}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[MLOps]}]: A production phenomenon where the true relationship between input features $\mathbf{X}$ and target $y$ changes over time ($P(y|\mathbf{X})$ shifts), degrading model accuracy.
\item[\textbf{Confidence Interval}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Statistics]}]: A statistical interval $[\bar{x} - ME, \bar{x} + ME]$ computed from sample data expected to contain the true population parameter with a chosen confidence level (e.g., $95\%$).
\item[\textbf{Confusion Matrix}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Evaluation]}]: A table cross-tabulating ground-truth labels against model predictions across True Positives, False Positives, True Negatives, and False Negatives.
\item[\textbf{Convolutional Neural Network (CNN)}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Deep Learning]}]: A specialized neural architecture utilizing shared parameter kernels and pooling operations to extract translation-invariant spatial and temporal features.
\item[\textbf{Cosine Similarity}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Mathematics]}]: A metric measuring the cosine of the angle between two vectors: $\cos(\theta) = \frac{\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v}}{\|\mathbf{u}\| \|\mathbf{v}\|}$, evaluating directional orientation irrespective of magnitude.
\item[\textbf{Cross-Validation}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Evaluation]}]: A resampling validation method partitioning data into $K$ folds, iteratively training on $K-1$ folds and testing on the remaining fold to assess out-of-sample generalization.
\end{description}

\section*{D}
\begin{description}[leftmargin=!,labelwidth=4.5cm,itemsep=1.5ex]
\item[\textbf{Data Augmentation}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Deep Learning]}]: Artificially inflating training set diversity by applying domain-preserving transformations (e.g., jitter, rotations, noise) without changing true semantic labels.
\item[\textbf{Data Drift}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[MLOps]}]: A production shift where input feature distributions change over time ($P(\mathbf{X})$ shifts) while the underlying relationship $P(y|\mathbf{X})$ remains unchanged.
\item[\textbf{Data Leakage}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Data Engineering]}]: A critical flaw occurring when information from outside the training partition (such as test labels or future timestamps) contaminates the model training process.
\item[\textbf{Decision Tree}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Classical ML]}]: A non-parametric model partitioning feature space into orthogonal rectangular regions via greedy recursive splits that maximize purity (Information Gain or Gini Impurity).
\item[\textbf{Deep Sleep}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[IoT \& Hardware]}]: An ultra-low-power microcontroller operating mode powering down CPU cores and radios, leaving only a real-time clock drawing microamp-level current ($10\,\mu\text{A}$ to $15\,\mu\text{A}$).
\item[\textbf{Depthwise Separable Convolution}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Deep Learning]}]: A factorization splitting standard convolution into depthwise spatial filtering per channel followed by $1 \times 1$ pointwise channel mixing, cutting compute by $8\times$ to $9\times$.
\item[\textbf{Derivative}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Mathematics]}]: The instantaneous rate of change of a function with respect to an independent variable: $f'(x) = \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{f(x+h) - f(x)}{h}$.
\item[\textbf{Dimensionality Reduction}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Classical ML]}]: Transforming high-dimensional data into a lower-dimensional space while preserving structural variance and relationships (e.g., via Principal Component Analysis).
\item[\textbf{Docker}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[MLOps]}]: An open-source containerization platform packaging software code, runtime dependencies, and system libraries into an immutable, portable software container.
\item[\textbf{Dot Product}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Mathematics]}]: An algebraic operation multiplying corresponding components of two vectors and summing the products: $\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v} = \sum u_i v_i = \|\mathbf{u}\| \|\mathbf{v}\| \cos(\theta)$.
\item[\textbf{Dropout}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Deep Learning]}]: A regularization technique randomly setting a fraction $p$ of hidden activations to zero during training passes, preventing co-adaptation of neurons.
\item[\textbf{Duty Cycling}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[IoT \& Hardware]}]: An energy-saving operating pattern alternating between short active bursts ($10\,\text{ms}$ to $50\,\text{ms}$) of sensing/inference and prolonged deep sleep, slashing average power by $98\%$.
\end{description}

\section*{E}
\begin{description}[leftmargin=!,labelwidth=4.5cm,itemsep=1.5ex]
\item[\textbf{Early Stopping}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Deep Learning]}]: Terminating neural network training automatically when validation loss fails to improve for a set number of consecutive epochs (patience), preventing overfitting.
\item[\textbf{Edge AI}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[TinyML]}]: Executing machine learning models locally on physical embedded devices and microcontrollers at the network periphery rather than routing data to central cloud servers.
\item[\textbf{Edge Impulse}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[TinyML Tools]}]: A developer platform providing automated pipelines for sensor data collection, DSP feature extraction, model training, integer quantization, and C++ library export.
\item[\textbf{Eigenvalue \& Eigenvector}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Mathematics]}]: An eigenvector $\mathbf{v}$ of matrix $\mathbf{A}$ maintains its spatial direction under linear transformation, scaling only by eigenvalue $\lambda$: $\mathbf{A}\mathbf{v} = \lambda \mathbf{v}$.
\item[\textbf{Embedded C++}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[IoT \& Software]}]: A disciplined C++ programming style avoiding dynamic heap allocation (\code{malloc}, \code{new}) and exceptions to ensure deterministic execution within strict microcontroller SRAM.
\item[\textbf{Ensemble Learning}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Classical ML]}]: Combining the predictions of multiple diverse models (via averaging, voting, or stacking) to achieve superior accuracy and lower variance than individual models.
\item[\textbf{Entropy}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Information Theory]}]: A fundamental measure of uncertainty or impurity in a probability distribution: $H(P) = -\sum p_i \log_2(p_i)$. A pure, single-outcome distribution has zero entropy.
\item[\textbf{Epoch}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Deep Learning]}]: One complete forward and backward optimization pass through the entire training dataset.
\item[\textbf{ESP32}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[IoT \& Hardware]}]: A low-cost, dual-core 32-bit microcontroller by Espressif ($240\,\text{MHz}$, $520\,\text{KB}$ SRAM, integrated Wi-Fi/BLE), standard for prototyping connected TinyML systems.
\item[\textbf{Euclidean Distance}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Mathematics]}]: The geometric straight-line distance between two vectors $\mathbf{p}$ and $\mathbf{q}$ in $n$-dimensional space: $d(\mathbf{p}, \mathbf{q}) = \sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^n (p_i - q_i)^2}$.
\item[\textbf{Exploding Gradient Problem}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Deep Learning]}]: A training pathology where backpropagated gradient magnitudes grow exponentially across layers, causing numerical overflow and parameter divergence.
\end{description}

\section*{F}
\begin{description}[leftmargin=!,labelwidth=4.5cm,itemsep=1.5ex]
\item[\textbf{False Alarm Rate (FAR)}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Evaluation]}]: The proportion of normal evaluation instances incorrectly flagged as anomalies: $\text{FAR} = FP / (FP + TN)$. In industrial IoT, high FAR causes alert fatigue.
\item[\textbf{False Negative (FN)}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Evaluation]}]: A classification error where an actual positive instance (e.g., bearing crack) is incorrectly classified as negative (normal).
\item[\textbf{False Positive (FP)}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Evaluation]}]: A classification error where an actual negative instance (e.g., healthy motor) is incorrectly flagged as positive (anomalous).
\item[\textbf{Fast Fourier Transform (FFT)}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Signal Processing]}]: An efficient algorithm computing the Discrete Fourier Transform in $\mathcal{O}(N \log N)$ operations, converting time-domain sensor waveforms into frequency spectra.
\item[\textbf{FastAPI}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[MLOps]}]: A modern, asynchronous Python web framework built on Starlette and Pydantic, widely used for deploying high-throughput model inference endpoints.
\item[\textbf{Feature Engineering}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Data Engineering]}]: The process of extracting, transforming, and constructing informative numerical variables from raw sensor telemetry to maximize model learning efficiency.
\item[\textbf{Feature Map}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Deep Learning]}]: The output tensor generated by convolving an input tensor with a learnable filter kernel, indicating detected spatial or temporal features.
\item[\textbf{Federated Learning}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Distributed ML]}]: A decentralized training paradigm where edge devices compute gradients on private local data and transmit only weight updates to a central server.
\item[\textbf{FINER Criteria}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Research]}]: A framework evaluating research questions: Feasible, Interesting, Novel, Ethical, and Relevant before committing substantial engineering time.
\item[\textbf{Fixed-Point Arithmetic}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[TinyML]}]: Simulating fractional real numbers using integer registers by fixing radix point position, enabling fast inference on microcontrollers lacking hardware FPUs.
\item[\textbf{Flash Memory (ROM)}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[IoT \& Hardware]}]: Non-volatile on-chip semiconductor memory retaining compiled firmware binaries and quantized model weights when power is disconnected ($4\,\text{MB}$ to $16\,\text{MB}$ on ESP32).
\item[\textbf{FlatBuffers}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[TinyML Tools]}]: A memory-efficient serialization format enabling TensorFlow Lite for Microcontrollers to read models directly from read-only Flash without copying or unpacking.
\item[\textbf{F1 Score}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Evaluation]}]: The harmonic mean of Precision and Recall: $\text{F1} = 2 \cdot \frac{\text{Precision} \cdot \text{Recall}}{\text{Precision} + \text{Recall}}$, balancing false alarms and missed detections.
\item[\textbf{FreeRTOS}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[IoT \& Hardware]}]: A market-leading open-source real-time operating system microkernel providing priority-based preemptive task scheduling and queues on embedded microcontrollers.
\end{description}

\section*{G}
\begin{description}[leftmargin=!,labelwidth=4.5cm,itemsep=1.5ex]
\item[\textbf{Gated Recurrent Unit (GRU)}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Deep Learning]}]: A streamlined recurrent cell using reset and update gates to regulate sequential information flow without maintaining a separate cell state memory.
\item[\textbf{Generalization}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Machine Learning]}]: The core capability of a trained machine learning model to accurately predict outcomes on novel, previously unobserved data drawn from the target distribution.
\item[\textbf{Generative Adversarial Network (GAN)}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Deep Learning]}]: A generative framework pitting a Generator network synthesizing synthetic data against a Discriminator network distinguishing real from synthetic samples.
\item[\textbf{Gini Impurity}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Classical ML]}]: A criterion measuring the probability of incorrect labeling under random assignment: $G = 1 - \sum p_k^2$, used for evaluating CART decision tree splits.
\item[\textbf{Global Average Pooling}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Deep Learning]}]: A pooling layer computing the average activation across spatial dimensions ($H \times W \to 1 \times 1$), drastically reducing parameters and replacing dense layers.
\item[\textbf{Google XYZ Formula}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Career]}]: A resume bullet formula: \emph{``Accomplished [X], as measured by [Y], by doing [Z],''} emphasizing measurable quantitative engineering outcomes.
\item[\textbf{Gradient}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Mathematics]}]: The vector of first-order partial derivatives of a scalar function: $\nabla f = [\frac{\partial f}{\partial x_1}, \dots, \frac{\partial f}{\partial x_d}]^T$, pointing along steepest ascent.
\item[\textbf{Gradient Boosting}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Classical ML]}]: An ensemble method training weak decision trees sequentially to predict the negative gradient (pseudo-residuals) of the loss function with respect to ensemble predictions.
\item[\textbf{Gradient Descent}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Optimization]}]: An iterative optimization algorithm updating parameter weights along the negative gradient direction: $\mathbf{w}_{t+1} = \mathbf{w}_t - \eta \nabla f(\mathbf{w}_t)$.
\item[\textbf{Graph Neural Network (GNN)}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Specialized Architectures]}]: A deep learning model operating directly on non-Euclidean graph topologies, updating node states via recursive neighborhood message passing.
\item[\textbf{Grid Search}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Hyperparameter Tuning]}]: A brute-force hyperparameter optimization technique training and evaluating models across every point in a pre-defined Cartesian parameter grid.
\end{description}

\section*{H}
\begin{description}[leftmargin=!,labelwidth=4.5cm,itemsep=1.5ex]
\item[\textbf{Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL)}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[TinyML \& Testing]}]: An automated testing setup where physical microcontrollers are flashed and stimulated with synthetic sensor data to measure real latency and memory execution.
\item[\textbf{Hidden State}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Deep Learning]}]: The internal memory vector $\mathbf{h}_t$ of a recurrent neural network summarizing historical temporal information observed up to time step $t$.
\item[\textbf{High-Pass Filter}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Signal Processing]}]: A filter passing signal frequencies higher than a cutoff while attenuating lower frequencies, commonly used to eliminate static gravity offsets from accelerometers.
\item[\textbf{Holdout Set}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Evaluation]}]: A clean dataset partition completely quarantined from training and tuning, used exclusively to evaluate final model generalization.
\item[\textbf{HTTP}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[IoT \& Networking]}]: Hypertext Transfer Protocol: the standard application-layer protocol for web services. In IoT, HTTP incurs high header overhead ($> 500$ bytes) relative to MQTT.
\item[\textbf{Hyperparameter}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Machine Learning]}]: An external structural configuration parameter (e.g., learning rate, tree depth, batch size) that cannot be estimated directly from training data.
\item[\textbf{Hyperplane}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Mathematics]}]: A flat affine subspace of dimension $d - 1$ in a $d$-dimensional space, serving as the linear decision boundary in linear models and SVMs.
\item[\textbf{Hysteresis}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[TinyML]}]: Employing separate activation and deactivation thresholds to prevent noisy sensor fluctuations from repeatedly triggering spurious alarm state toggles.
\end{description}

\section*{I}
\begin{description}[leftmargin=!,labelwidth=4.5cm,itemsep=1.5ex]
\item[\textbf{I2C}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[IoT \& Hardware]}]: Inter-Integrated Circuit: a synchronous two-wire serial communication bus (SDA and SCL) standard for reading sensors like the MPU-6050 at $100\,\text{kHz}$ or $400\,\text{kHz}$.
\item[\textbf{Imbalanced Data}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Data Engineering]}]: A dataset condition where class frequencies are severely skewed (e.g., $99.9\%$ normal and $0.1\%$ failure), requiring balanced metrics like Macro-F1.
\item[\textbf{Imputation}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Data Engineering]}]: Replacing missing values in a dataset with estimated statistics (mean, median, mode, or KNN predictions) to prevent downstream model failure.
\item[\textbf{Inductive Bias}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Machine Learning]}]: The set of structural assumptions and mathematical constraints an algorithm uses to prioritize hypotheses and generalize beyond observed samples.
\item[\textbf{Inference}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Machine Learning]}]: The operational deployment phase where a trained model processes unobserved inputs through its forward pass to generate predictions.
\item[\textbf{Information Gain}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Classical ML]}]: The expected reduction in entropy achieved by partitioning a dataset on a feature: $\text{IG}(S, A) = H(S) - \sum \frac{|S_v|}{|S|} H(S_v)$.
\item[\textbf{In-place Operation}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Software Engineering]}]: A computation modifying data directly in its existing memory buffer without allocating secondary memory, essential within microcontroller SRAM limits.
\item[\textbf{Integer Quantization (Int8)}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[TinyML]}]: Converting 32-bit floating-point weights and activations to signed 8-bit integers ($-128$ to $+127$), achieving $4\times$ memory reduction and hardware SIMD speedup.
\item[\textbf{Internal SRAM}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[IoT \& Hardware]}]: Fast on-chip Static RAM ($520\,\text{KB}$ on ESP32) holding runtime stacks, heaps, and activation tensor arenas, with zero wait-state access.
\item[\textbf{Interrupt Service Routine (ISR)}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[IoT \& Hardware]}]: A prioritized embedded function executing immediately upon hardware trigger events (e.g., timer alarm, sensor pin threshold), pausing main CPU execution.
\item[\textbf{Inverted Residual Block}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Deep Learning]}]: The MobileNetV2 building block using narrow-wide-narrow bottlenecks with depthwise separable convolutions and residual skip connections between narrow bottlenecks.
\item[\textbf{Intersection over Union (IoU)}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Computer Vision]}]: The ratio of overlapping area to total union area between predicted and ground-truth bounding boxes: $\text{IoU} = \frac{\text{Area}(B_p \cap B_g)}{\text{Area}(B_p \cup B_g)}$.
\end{description}

\section*{J}
\begin{description}[leftmargin=!,labelwidth=4.5cm,itemsep=1.5ex]
\item[\textbf{Jaccard Similarity}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Mathematics]}]: A set similarity metric measuring the size of intersection divided by the size of union: $J(A, B) = \frac{|A \cap B|}{|A \cup B|}$.
\item[\textbf{Jacobian Matrix}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Mathematics]}]: The matrix of all first-order partial derivatives of a vector-valued multivariable function $\mathbf{f}: \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}^m$.
\item[\textbf{Jitter}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[IoT \& Networking]}]: Statistical variation or fluctuation in arrival times of communication packets or sensor clock intervals, stabilized via hardware timers.

\item[\textbf{Specificity}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Evaluation]}]: The True Negative Rate: the proportion of actual negative instances correctly identified as negative: $\text{Specificity} = TN / (TN + FP) = 1 - \text{FPR}$.

\item[\textbf{Stacking}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Classical ML]}]: An ensemble method using predictions from diverse base models as input features to train a meta-model that learns to combine their outputs optimally.
\item[\textbf{Standard Deviation}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Statistics]}]: The square root of variance, measuring the statistical dispersion of data points around their arithmetic mean: $\sigma = \sqrt{\frac{1}{N} \sum (x_i - \mu)^2}$.
\item[\textbf{Standard Error (SE)}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Statistics]}]: The standard deviation of a sample mean estimate: $SE = s / \sqrt{N}$, quantifying sampling uncertainty around the population mean.
\item[\textbf{Stationarity}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Time Series]}]: A property of time series whose statistical parameters (mean, variance, autocorrelation) do not change over time, a prerequisite for fitting ARIMA models.
\item[\textbf{Stochastic Gradient Descent (SGD)}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Optimization]}]: An optimization algorithm updating weights using a single randomly selected sample per iteration: $\mathbf{w} \leftarrow \mathbf{w} - \eta \nabla \mathcal{L}_i(\mathbf{w})$.
\item[\textbf{Stratified Sampling}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Data Engineering]}]: A dataset partitioning strategy ensuring each split (train, validation, test) contains the exact same percentage proportion of target classes as the full dataset.
\item[\textbf{Student's t-Distribution}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Statistics]}]: A continuous symmetric probability distribution used when estimating the population mean from small samples ($N < 30$) with unknown population variance.
\item[\textbf{Supervised Learning}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Machine Learning]}]: The machine learning paradigm where models are trained on labeled pairs $(\mathbf{x}_i, y_i)$ to learn an optimal mapping function $f: \mathbf{x} \to y$.
\item[\textbf{Support Vector Machine (SVM)}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Classical ML]}]: A supervised algorithm finding the maximum-margin hyperplane separating classes in feature space, supported by boundary instances called support vectors.
\item[\textbf{Symmetric Quantization}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[TinyML]}]: Uniform integer quantization fixing the zero-point to zero ($Z = 0$), mapping floating ranges symmetrically around zero to signed integers ($[-127, +127]$).
\end{description}

\section*{T}
\begin{description}[leftmargin=!,labelwidth=4.5cm,itemsep=1.5ex]
\item[\textbf{Target Encoding}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Data Engineering]}]: Replacing categorical values with the mean of the continuous target variable for that category across the training set, requiring out-of-fold regularization.
\item[\textbf{Temperature (Softmax)}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Deep Learning]}]: A scalar $T$ scaling logits before Softmax: $\text{softmax}(z_i / T)$. Lower $T < 1$ sharpens predictions; higher $T > 1$ softens distributions toward uniform entropy.
\item[\textbf{Tensor}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Mathematics]}]: A multi-dimensional geometric array of numerical values generalizing scalars (rank 0), vectors (rank 1), and matrices (rank 2) to arbitrary ranks.
\item[\textbf{Tensor Arena}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[TinyML]}]: A contiguous, statically allocated byte buffer in microcontroller SRAM reserved by TFLM to hold intermediate activations and runtime scratch tensors.
\item[\textbf{TensorFlow Lite (TFLite)}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[TinyML Tools]}]: Google's lightweight runtime framework executing quantized, optimized neural network models on mobile and edge devices.
\item[\textbf{TensorFlow Lite for Microcontrollers (TFLM)}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[TinyML Tools]}]: A bare-metal C++ port of TFLite executing models in tens of kilobytes of SRAM on microcontrollers without dynamic memory allocation or operating systems.
\item[\textbf{Time-Series Forecasting}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Time Series]}]: The machine learning task of analyzing historical chronological sensor telemetry to predict future numeric values across a forward time horizon.
\item[\textbf{TinyML}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[TinyML]}]: The engineering discipline of executing machine learning models on resource-constrained microcontrollers operating under milliwatt power and sub-megabyte memory limits.
\item[\textbf{Tokenization}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[NLP]}]: Segmenting raw text into discrete linguistic units (tokens) such as words or subwords and mapping them to integer IDs for neural model ingestion.
\item[\textbf{Transfer Learning}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Deep Learning]}]: Fine-tuning representations learned by a neural network trained on a massive source dataset (e.g., ImageNet) to solve a smaller target task.
\item[\textbf{Transformer}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Deep Learning]}]: A neural network architecture based entirely on self-attention mechanisms and feedforward layers, processing sequence tokens in parallel without recurrence.
\item[\textbf{True Negative (TN)}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Evaluation]}]: A classification outcome where a model correctly predicts the negative class for an evaluation instance whose true label is negative.
\item[\textbf{True Positive (TP)}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Evaluation]}]: A classification outcome where a model correctly predicts the positive class for an evaluation instance whose true label is positive.
\item[\textbf{Two-Phase Transfer Learning}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Deep Learning]}]: A transfer learning training regimen initially training only the new classification head with a frozen backbone (Phase 1), followed by unfreezing top backbone layers with a low learning rate (Phase 2).
\end{description}

\section*{U}
\begin{description}[leftmargin=!,labelwidth=4.5cm,itemsep=1.5ex]
\item[\textbf{UART}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[IoT \& Hardware]}]: Universal Asynchronous Receiver-Transmitter: a simple hardware serial communication protocol operating over TX/RX lines without a shared clock line, standard for flashing firmware.
\item[\textbf{U-Net}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Deep Learning]}]: An encoder-decoder convolutional architecture with horizontal skip connections between contracting and expanding paths, standard for semantic image segmentation.
\item[\textbf{Underfitting}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Machine Learning]}]: A modeling failure where an algorithm is too simplistic to capture the underlying structure of data, resulting in poor training and validation accuracy.
\item[\textbf{Uniform Distribution}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Probability]}]: A probability distribution where every possible outcome in an interval $[a, b]$ has equal likelihood of occurrence: $f(x) = \frac{1}{b - a}$.
\item[\textbf{Unit Testing}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Software Engineering]}]: Writing automated, isolated tests (e.g., with \code{pytest}) to verify that individual modular functions, transforms, and tensor shapes execute correctly.
\item[\textbf{Unsupervised Learning}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Machine Learning]}]: A machine learning paradigm discovering patterns, structures, and groupings from unlabeled data without human target supervision.
\item[\textbf{Uvicorn}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[MLOps]}]: A fast, lightweight Asynchronous Server Gateway Interface (ASGI) web server implementation in Python, standard for serving FastAPI model microservices.
\end{description}

\section*{V}
\begin{description}[leftmargin=!,labelwidth=4.5cm,itemsep=1.5ex]
\item[\textbf{Validation Set}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Evaluation]}]: A data partition separate from the training set used iteratively during development to tune hyperparameters and detect overfitting.
\item[\textbf{Vanishing Gradient Problem}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Deep Learning]}]: A training failure in deep networks where backpropagated gradients shrink exponentially through layers, leaving early layer weights unable to update.
\item[\textbf{Variance}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Statistics]}]: The expected squared deviation of a random variable from its arithmetic mean: $\sigma^2 = \mathbb{E}[(X - \mu)^2]$, measuring statistical dispersion.
\item[\textbf{Vector}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Mathematics]}]: A one-dimensional ordered array of numerical values representing a geometric magnitude and direction in space: $\mathbf{x} \in \mathbb{R}^d$.

\item[\textbf{Virtual Environment}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Software Engineering]}]: An isolated directory tree containing a specific Python interpreter and installed packages, preventing cross-project dependency conflicts.
\item[\textbf{Volatile Memory}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[IoT \& Hardware]}]: Computer memory (such as SRAM or DRAM) requiring continuous electrical power to retain data; all contents are lost when power is disconnected.

\section*{W}
\begin{description}[leftmargin=!,labelwidth=4.5cm,itemsep=1.5ex]
\item[\textbf{Weight Decay}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Deep Learning]}]: A regularization technique shrinking weights toward zero during gradient descent: $\mathbf{w}_{t+1} = (1 - \eta \lambda) \mathbf{w}_t - \eta \nabla \mathcal{L}$, mathematically equivalent to L2 regularization.
\item[\textbf{Weighted Sum Model (WSM)}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Decision Analysis]}]: A multi-criteria decision analysis method scoring alternative choices across weighted criteria to compute overall utility: $U = \mathbf{w}^T \mathbf{r}$.

\item[\textbf{Windowing}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[Signal Processing]}]: Segmenting continuous temporal sensor streams into fixed-length discrete time slices (e.g., 128 samples with $50\%$ stride) for feature extraction.
\item[\textbf{Word Embedding}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[NLP]}]: A dense, low-dimensional continuous vector representation of words where semantically similar words are positioned in close geometric proximity.
\item[\textbf{Word2Vec}\hfill \textnormal{\sffamily\scriptsize\color{keyideablue}[NLP]}]: An unsupervised algorithm learning word embeddings by predicting context words from a target word (Skip-gram) or predicting a target from context (CBOW).
\end{description}
